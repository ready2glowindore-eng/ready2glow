How your estimated total works

  1. Tap Add on each service you want. You can mix categories – for example, a cleanup, eyebrow threading and full arms waxing in one visit.
  2. The estimated total updates as you add or remove services.
  3. When you tap Book Now, your services and the estimated total go into your WhatsApp message, so there's no confusion about what you asked for.
  4. Our team replies on WhatsApp to confirm the price, beautician and time.

Questions about payment or anything else before you book? Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll answer before you confirm.
