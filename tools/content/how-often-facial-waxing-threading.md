| Service | How often | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cleanup | Every 3–4 weeks | Keeps pores clear. Ideal as your monthly routine |
| Facial (Fruit / Gold) | Once a month, or before occasions | For glow and relaxation. Book 2–3 days before an event |

| Eyebrow threading | Every 2–3 weeks | Keeps your shape before regrowth blurs it |

*These are general guidelines. Your skin, hair growth and lifestyle will shift them a little, so adjust after a couple of cycles.*

## Facials: how often is right?

**For most women, a cleanup every 3–4 weeks plus a facial once a month or before occasions is plenty.** Booking weekly facials rarely adds benefit and can irritate sensitive skin.

### How often should you get a facial in your 20s, 30s and 40s?

- **20s:** a cleanup every 3–4 weeks is usually enough. Add a facial before events.
- **30s and 40s:** many women move to a monthly facial because skin tends to look duller and drier. A Fruit Facial (about 60 minutes) is gentle for regular use.
- **Any age with sensitive or acne-prone skin:** keep it simple and mention it in your booking notes. For active acne or a skin condition, follow your dermatologist's advice on frequency.

## Waxing: every 3–4 weeks

Waxing results usually last **3–4 weeks**. Book again when the hair is about **4–6 mm** long. If you wax too soon, short hairs get missed. If you wait too long, it can feel more uncomfortable.

With regular waxing, many women find regrowth gets slower and finer over time, and the appointment gets more comfortable. Avoid shaving between sessions, because it resets the cycle. Our [waxing before-and-after guide](/blog/waxing-before-and-after-care/) covers preparation, ingrown hairs and aftercare, and you can [book waxing at home](/waxing-at-home-indore/) when you're due.

## Threading: every 2–3 weeks for brows

Eyebrows usually need threading **every 2–3 weeks** to keep their shape. Upper lip and full face threading can often stretch a bit longer, depending on your hair growth. Threading is quick (eyebrows about 10 minutes, upper lip about 5), so it's easy to add [eyebrow threading at home](/threading-at-home-indore/) to any other visit.

## Manicure and pedicure

- **Manicure:** as a general guide, every 2–3 weeks, as polish and cuticles grow out.
- **Pedicure:** every 3–4 weeks. In winter, or if your heels crack easily, a Spa Pedicure with a mask and longer massage gives extra care.

Between visits, moisturise your hands and feet daily and wear gloves while washing dishes. See [manicure and pedicure at home](/manicure-pedicure-at-home-indore/).

## Hair spa: every 3–4 weeks

A hair spa **every 3–4 weeks** suits most hair types. If your hair is very dry, frizzy or damaged from heat styling, you might go a little more often. Avoid heat styling for 24 hours afterwards and use a mild, sulphate-free shampoo to make the results last. More on [hair spa at home](/hair-spa-at-home-indore/).

## A simple monthly beauty routine (two home visits)

Instead of booking ten separate appointments, group services into one or two visits a month.

**Visit 1 (start of the month), about 2.5–3 hours:**

- Cleanup or facial
- Waxing (arms, legs, underarms as needed)
- Eyebrow and upper lip threading
- Pedicure

**Visit 2 (mid-month), about 30–60 minutes:**

- Eyebrow threading touch-up
- Manicure, or hair spa if it's due

## Adjusting for events and seasons

- **Before a function:** facial 2–3 days before; waxing about a week before; threading 1–2 days before. Planning a wedding? Use our [pre-wedding beauty timeline](/blog/pre-wedding-beauty-timeline/).
- **After summer or travel:** add a D-Tan Pack (about 30 minutes) to your cleanup visit.
- **Winter:** dry skin and cracked heels mean pedicures and moisturising matter more.
- **Festival season:** book early. [Makeup](/makeup-artist-at-home-indore/) slots for festival dates should be booked 3–7 days ahead.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it bad to get a facial every week?

For most people, there's no benefit to weekly facials, and frequent scrubbing and steaming can irritate the skin. Monthly, or a cleanup every 3–4 weeks, is enough.

### How long does a full leg wax last?

Usually 3–4 weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows.

### How often should I get my eyebrows threaded?

Every 2–3 weeks keeps them in shape.

### Can I book all my monthly services in one home visit?

Yes. Add all the services you want to one booking request and the beautician does them in a single appointment.
