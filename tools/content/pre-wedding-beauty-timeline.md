## Why timing matters

A facial the day before can leave your skin pink. Waxing too late can leave bumps. A first-ever eyebrow shape on the wedding eve is a gamble. Booking at home also means you don't have to run between parlours while the house is full of relatives.

## The short version

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 6–8 weeks before | Start a regular cleanup and settle on your brow shape |
| 4 weeks before | Facial or cleanup, waxing, hair spa |
| 2 weeks before | Plan your looks, second hair spa, eyebrow threading |
| 1 week before | Final waxing, manicure, spa pedicure |
| 2–3 days before | Gold facial, final threading |
| Wedding day | Makeup and saree draping |

Now the detail.

## 6–8 weeks before: build the base

- **Start a cleanup every 3–4 weeks.** Regular cleanups keep pores clear so your skin is calm and predictable closer to the date. If you're unsure whether you need a cleanup or a facial, read [cleanup vs facial](/blog/cleanup-vs-facial/).
- **Settle your eyebrow shape.** If you want a new shape, start now. Brows usually need threading every 2–3 weeks, so this gives you two or three sessions to perfect it.
- **Stop shaving if you plan to wax.** Hair needs about 4–6 mm of growth (2–3 weeks) for waxing to work well.
- **See a dermatologist now** if you have acne, pigmentation or any skin condition you want treated. Medical treatments need time, and this isn't the stage to experiment.

## 4 weeks before: first full grooming round

- **Facial or cleanup.** A Fruit Facial (₹699, about 60 minutes) is gentle and suits most skin types.
- **Full body waxing** (₹1,499, about 90 minutes), or full arms (₹299) and full legs (₹449). This "practice round" shows you how your skin reacts, so there are no surprises later. Follow our [waxing before-and-after care guide](/blog/waxing-before-and-after-care/).
- **Hair spa** (₹799, about 60 minutes). A deep-conditioning treatment for dry or frizzy hair. A hair spa every 3–4 weeks works well for most hair types.

## 2 weeks before: fine-tune

- **Eyebrow threading** to maintain the shape you've settled on.
- **Second hair spa** if your hair is very dry.
- **Plan your look.** Decide your outfits and jewellery for each function so your makeup artist can match them.
- **Book your function-day services now.** For weddings and festival dates, book makeup 3–7 days ahead at the latest, and earlier in the wedding season.

## 1 week before: hands, feet and final waxing

- **Final waxing.** Doing it about a week before gives any redness or bumps time to settle before the functions.
- **Manicure** (₹449, about 40 minutes) for neat, soft hands before the mehendi.
- **Spa pedicure** (₹799, about 60 minutes). A pedicure with a mask and longer massage. Your feet will be on show with sandals and payal.

**Tip:** If you're having mehendi, plan your manicure before the mehendi function, not after.

## 2–3 days before: the glow

- **Gold Facial** (₹999, about 75 minutes) is a popular choice 2–3 days before weddings for a radiant finish. **Don't get a facial on the wedding day or the day before.** Give your skin time to settle.
- **Final threading:** eyebrows, upper lip or full face (₹149, about 20 minutes).
- **Head massage** (₹299, about 30 minutes) if you want something relaxing for the nerves.

After the facial, avoid direct sun and heavy makeup for 24 hours, and skip scrubs for 2 days.

## Pre-wedding beauty checklist

- Brow shape decided 6+ weeks out
- Shaving stopped 3 weeks before the first wax
- Practice waxing round done 4 weeks out
- Makeup and draping booked for every function
- Final waxing about 1 week before
- Manicure before mehendi
- Gold facial 2–3 days before, never the same day
- Any skin allergies noted in the booking form
- Bed or reclining chair, pillow and water ready for each home visit

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I start my pre-bridal routine?

Ideally 6–8 weeks before the wedding, with regular cleanups and brow shaping. The main services (waxing, manicure, pedicure, facial) happen in the final 1–2 weeks.

### When should I get a facial before the wedding?

2–3 days before. A facial on the same day or the day before can leave skin slightly red.

### When should I wax before the wedding?

About a week before is a safe choice, so any redness or bumps settle. Do a practice round about 4 weeks earlier if you're new to waxing.

### Can all of this be done at home?

Yes. Facials, waxing, threading, manicure, pedicure, hair spa, party makeup and saree draping are all available as home services for women in Indore.
