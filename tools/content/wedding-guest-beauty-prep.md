## About a week before: waxing and hands and feet

- **Waxing:** book it about a week before, so any redness or small bumps settle before you wear a sleeveless blouse or lehenga. Don't shave in between. Read [how to prepare for waxing](/blog/waxing-before-and-after-care/).
- **Manicure and pedicure:** your hands show in every photo, especially with mehendi and bangles. A Spa Pedicure is worth it in winter if your heels are dry. See [manicure vs pedicure vs spa pedicure](/blog/manicure-vs-pedicure-vs-spa-pedicure/).

**Tip:** if you're getting mehendi, do the manicure **before** the mehendi, not after.

## 2–3 days before: your facial

A **Gold Facial** is the popular pre-function choice. A **Fruit Facial** works well too if you get facials regularly. **Don't get a facial on the day of the wedding or the day before.** Skin can look a little pink after steam and massage, and it needs time to settle. Not sure which to pick? Read [gold facial vs fruit facial](/blog/gold-facial-vs-fruit-facial/).

## 1–2 days before: threading

Thread your eyebrows and upper lip 1–2 days before, not on the day, so any redness is gone. If you want a new brow shape, try it a few weeks earlier, not just before a wedding. See [threading vs waxing for the face](/blog/threading-vs-waxing-face/).

## The function day: makeup and saree draping at home

Getting ready at home means no travel in your outfit, no parlour queue in wedding season, and more time with family.

**Party Makeup (₹1,999, about 90 minutes)** includes:

1. A chat about your outfit and the look you want
2. Skin prep and base
3. Eyes, contour and lips
4. Setting for long wear

**Saree Draping (₹499)** comes after makeup, so nothing smudges on the fabric. Have your saree, a well-fitted blouse and petticoat ready, plus a few safety pins. More on [saree draping at home](/saree-draping-at-home-indore/).

### Wedding guest makeup looks in a saree

A few simple ideas to discuss with your makeup artist:

- **Day functions (haldi, mehendi):** a lighter, fresher look with soft eyes and a natural lip
- **Evening functions (sangeet, reception):** more defined eyes, a bolder lip, and makeup set for a long night
- **Match, don't compete:** pick one feature to highlight, eyes or lips, based on your outfit's colours

Share a photo of your outfit when you book. It helps plan the look.

## Getting ready with family

If several women in the family need makeup or draping, add the services for each person to one booking and mention the number of people in the additional instructions. Our team will confirm on WhatsApp how many people can be done for your time slot.

## Booking tips for wedding season

- **Book 3–7 days ahead** for wedding and festival dates. Popular dates fill up quickly.
- Our appointments run **8 AM to 8 PM**. For an early function, tell us your "ready by" time when you book and we'll confirm what's possible.
- **Count backwards from when you need to leave.** Makeup takes about 90 minutes, and draping comes after it.
- **Keep the day simple.** Only makeup and draping on the function day, nothing new on your skin.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I get a facial before attending a wedding?

2–3 days before. Avoid the wedding day and the day before, because skin can look slightly pink after a facial.

### How early should I book makeup for a wedding?

3–7 days ahead for wedding and festival dates. On other days, 1–2 days' notice is usually enough.

### Can you do makeup and saree draping for more than one person?

Yes. Add the services for each person to your booking and mention the number of people. Our team will confirm timing on WhatsApp.

### Should I wax or thread on the day of the wedding?

No. Wax about a week before and thread 1–2 days before, so any redness settles.

### Do I need to buy makeup products?

No. The makeup artist brings professional products. If you have allergies or prefer your own products, mention it when you book.
