## The real benefits of salon services at home

### 1. You save the time you'd lose in between

A 45-minute cleanup at a parlour often turns into two hours once you add travel, parking and waiting. At home, the appointment takes about as long as the service itself.

### 2. Privacy and comfort

Waxing, especially full body waxing (about 90 minutes), is something many women prefer to do in their own room rather than a shared cubicle. You also get to relax afterwards instead of travelling home with freshly waxed skin or a just-done facial in the dust.

### 3. You can watch the hygiene

At home you see everything: whether strips are fresh, whether a spatula goes back into the wax, whether tools come out sanitised. At Ready2Glow, beauticians use fresh strips and spatulas for every customer, never double-dip, use a fresh thread for every customer, and sanitise metal tools before every appointment. For a full checklist of what to watch, read our [home salon hygiene and safety guide](/blog/is-home-salon-service-safe/).

### 4. Easier for mothers, elders and busy schedules

If you have a small child, an elderly parent at home, or back-to-back work calls, home service lets you fit beauty care around your day instead of the other way round.

### 5. Combine several services in one visit

Threading, waxing, a cleanup and a pedicure can all happen in one booked visit. Packages like Glow Essentials (cleanup, eyebrow and upper lip threading, full arms waxing) or Monthly Pamper (fruit facial, full legs waxing, manicure, pedicure) are designed for exactly that. ### 6. Ideal for functions

Before a wedding or festival, the beautician can do [party makeup and saree draping at home](/makeup-artist-at-home-indore/) while you get ready with your family, with no rushing across the city in your outfit.

## When a beauty parlour is the better choice

Home service isn't right for everything. A parlour usually makes more sense when:

- **You need a hair wash basin and professional dryers.** This includes hair colour, smoothening, keratin and other chemical treatments that need fixed equipment and a well-ventilated space. These aren't part of Ready2Glow's home service menu.
- **You want a machine-based treatment** such as hydra facials or laser procedures, which need equipment that isn't portable. Laser and similar procedures should be done under proper medical supervision.
- **You don't have the space.** If there's no bed, reclining chair or quiet corner at home, a parlour will be more comfortable.
- **You enjoy the outing.** For some women, a parlour visit with a friend is part of the fun, and that's a perfectly good reason.

## What you need at home

Very little. The beautician brings the products, towels and disposable items. You arrange:

- A bed or reclining chair, and a pillow for facials
- Access to water (warm water for pedicures)
- A well-lit spot with a little space around it
