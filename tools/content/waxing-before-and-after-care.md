## Before waxing: the checklist

### 1. Let the hair grow to the right length

Hair should be about **4–6 mm long**, roughly 2–3 weeks of growth after shaving. If it's too short, the wax can't grip it and you'll be left with stubble. If it's very long, waxing can be more uncomfortable, so it's fine to ask your beautician to trim it first.

If you usually shave, stop shaving the area for 2–3 weeks before your first waxing appointment.

### 2. Exfoliate gently, 1–2 days before

A gentle scrub or loofah a day or two before helps lift dead skin, so hair comes out more cleanly. **Don't scrub on the day of waxing.** Freshly scrubbed skin is more sensitive.

### 3. Keep skin clean and dry on the day

- Shower before the appointment if you can.
- **Skip body lotion, oil and moisturiser** on the area to be waxed that day. Oily skin stops wax from gripping properly.
- Skip deodorant before underarm waxing.

### 4. Things to avoid before waxing

- **Sunburn or a fresh tan.** Wait until the skin settles.
- **Cuts, rashes, active pimples or irritated skin** on the area. Tell your beautician so that patch can be avoided.
- **Strong skincare on the area.** If you use retinoids or strong acids, or are on medication that makes skin fragile, check with your doctor before waxing that area.

### 5. Tell us what we need to know

Mention sensitive skin, allergies or a past reaction in the **Additional Instructions** box of the booking form. That helps the beautician take extra care.

### 6. Set up your space (for home waxing)

The beautician brings the wax, strips, spatulas and post-wax lotion. Keep ready:

- A bed, or a chair for arms and legs, with enough room around it
- Access to water
- Loose, comfortable clothes to change into afterwards

## Preparing by area

### Underarms

Skip deodorant, roll-on or powder that morning. Wear a loose, sleeveless or short-sleeved top so the area stays uncovered after waxing. Underarm waxing takes about 10 minutes.

### Legs

Stop shaving 2–3 weeks before. Keep loose trousers or a skirt to change into afterwards. Tight jeans right after waxing can rub and irritate the skin. Full legs waxing takes about 40 minutes.

### Arms

Remove bangles, watches and rings beforehand. Full arms waxing takes about 30 minutes.

### Full body

Plan for about 90 minutes and keep the rest of the day free of the gym or swimming.

## Does waxing hurt? Small things that help

There is brief discomfort, and for most women it reduces with regular waxing. Things that can help:

- **Book regularly.** Many women find hair grows back finer over time with consistent waxing.
- **Breathe out slowly** as each strip comes off.
- **Avoid scheduling during or just before your period** if you notice your skin is more sensitive then.
- **Stay relaxed and keep the skin taut.** Your beautician will guide you.

## After waxing: the first 24 hours

These are the rules that matter most:

- **No hot showers, saunas, steam or swimming for 24 hours.** Heat and chlorine can irritate open pores.
- **No deodorant or perfume on freshly waxed skin for a few hours.** This matters most for underarms.
- **Wear loose, breathable clothing** to reduce friction.
- **Avoid direct sun and tanning.**
- **Skip the gym** if you can. Sweat on freshly waxed skin can cause bumps.
- **Don't scratch or touch the area much.**

Mild redness or small bumps right after waxing are common and usually settle within a few hours. A little aloe vera gel or a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser can soothe the skin. If you notice significant swelling, blistering or signs of infection, see a doctor.

## How to prevent ingrown hairs after waxing

Ingrown hairs happen when a regrowing hair curls back into the skin instead of coming out. To reduce them:

- **Exfoliate gently 2–3 days after waxing**, then 2–3 times a week.
- **Moisturise daily** so the skin stays soft and hair can push through.
- **Wear loose clothing** in the days after waxing, especially on the legs.
- **Don't shave between waxing sessions.** Shaving cuts hair bluntly and resets the cycle.
- **Don't pick at ingrown hairs.** Exfoliate gently instead, and see a dermatologist if one becomes painful or infected.

## What to do after waxing to avoid strawberry legs

"Strawberry legs" are the dark dots some women see on their legs. They're often clogged or darkened pores and hair follicles. Waxing usually helps more than shaving, but to keep them in check:

- Follow the 24-hour rules above, especially avoiding heat, sweat and tight clothes.
- Exfoliate gently 2–3 times a week once the skin has settled.
- Moisturise daily with a light, non-greasy lotion.
- Stick to waxing rather than switching back to shaving.

If the dots don't improve, a dermatologist can tell you whether something else is going on.

## Underarm aftercare

- No deodorant or antiperspirant for a few hours, ideally until the next morning.
- Wear a loose, cotton top.
- Avoid the gym and heavy sweating that day.
- If the skin feels warm, a cool (not ice-cold) compress can help.

## How long do results last?

Usually **3–4 weeks**, depending on how fast your hair grows. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long should hair be before waxing?

About 4–6 mm, which is roughly 2–3 weeks of growth after shaving.

### Can I apply moisturiser before waxing?

Not on the day. Lotion and oil stop the wax from gripping. Moisturise the day before instead.

### When can I exfoliate after waxing?

Wait 2–3 days, then exfoliate gently 2–3 times a week to help prevent ingrown hairs.
