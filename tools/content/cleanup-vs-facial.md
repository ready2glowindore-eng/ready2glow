## Cleanup or facial me kya antar hai?

Simple bhasha mein: **cleanup = safai, facial = safai + poshan (nourishment)**.

A cleanup is like a deep wash for your face. It unclogs pores and leaves skin fresh, but it doesn't include a long massage. A facial adds a massage with facial cream and a nourishing pack, which is why it takes longer and costs more. If your skin just feels oily or dusty, a cleanup is usually enough. If it looks dull and tired, or you have a function coming up, choose a facial.

## What happens in a cleanup

A Ready2Glow cleanup at home takes about 45 minutes:

1. **Cleansing** to remove surface dirt, oil and sunscreen.
2. **Scrub** to lift dead skin cells.
3. **Steam** to soften the skin and open pores.
4. **Mask** to calm and refresh the skin.

It's quick and practical, and easy to fit into a weekday evening slot. Many women keep a cleanup as their monthly routine and save facials for occasions.

## What happens in a facial

A facial follows the same base steps and then goes further:

1. Skin check and cleansing
2. Scrub and steam to open pores
3. **Massage with facial cream**, the step that makes a facial feel relaxing
4. **Face pack or mask** suited to the facial type
5. Toner and moisturiser to finish

**Fruit Facial (₹699, about 60 min):** gentle and refreshing, and suitable for most skin types. A good choice when your skin looks dull or tired.

**Gold Facial (₹999, about 75 min):** a popular pick 2–3 days before weddings, parties and festivals, for a radiant finish.

## Cleanup or facial: which is better?

Neither is "better". They do different jobs. Use these simple rules:

- **Choose a cleanup if** you want regular upkeep, your skin feels oily or clogged, you're short on time, or you had a facial recently.
- **Choose a facial if** your skin looks dull or tired, you want a relaxing session, or you have an event in the next few days.
- **Choose a Gold Facial if** it's for a wedding, reception or festival. Book it 2–3 days before the event, not on the same day.

### Cleanup or facial for oily skin?

For oily skin, a regular cleanup is often the more practical choice because it focuses on clearing oil and dead skin from pores. You can still enjoy a facial before events. Just mention that your skin is oily in your booking notes. If you have active acne, painful breakouts or a skin condition, ask a dermatologist first.

### For sensitive skin

Mention sensitivity, allergies or acne in the booking form's **Additional Instructions**, so the beautician can choose suitable products and skip harsh steps. If you've reacted to a product before, tell us its name.

## How often should you get a cleanup or facial?

- **Cleanup:** every 3–4 weeks works for most people.
- **Facial:** once a month, or before special occasions.

## How to prepare for a facial or cleanup at home

You don't need to arrange much, because the beautician brings the products, towels and disposable items. Just keep ready:

- **A bed or reclining chair and a pillow**, so you can lie back comfortably
- **Access to water**
- **A clean face if possible.** Remove heavy makeup beforehand if you can.
- **Good light near where you'll lie down.** It helps the beautician see your skin clearly.

A few more tips:

- Don't use a scrub or strong exfoliating products the day before.
- If you also want threading, add it to the same visit. Many women add [eyebrow or upper lip threading](/threading-at-home-indore/) to a cleanup or facial.
- Avoid booking a facial on the same day as an event. Skin can look a little pink right after, so 2–3 days before is ideal. Getting ready for a wedding? See our [pre-wedding beauty timeline](/blog/pre-wedding-beauty-timeline/).

## Aftercare: first 24–48 hours

- Avoid direct sun and heavy makeup for 24 hours.
- Skip scrubs and face washes with strong actives for 2 days.
- Drink plenty of water and keep your skin moisturised.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a cleanup enough, or do I need a facial every month?

For routine care, a cleanup every 3–4 weeks is enough for most people. Add a facial when your skin looks dull or before an event.

### Can I get a cleanup and D-Tan together?

Yes. Both can be booked in the same home appointment. Add both services before you send your booking request.

### Which facial should I book before a wedding?

Gold Facial is a popular choice. Book it 2–3 days before the function so any redness settles and your skin looks its best on the day.

### How long does a home facial take?

About 45 minutes for a cleanup, about 60 minutes for a Fruit Facial and about 75 minutes for a Gold Facial.
