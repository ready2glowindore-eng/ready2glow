Ready2Glow is a home salon service for women in Indore. Booking takes about a minute and happens on WhatsApp. Here are the five steps.

## Step 1: Choose your services

Go to the [home salon services on our homepage](/) or open any service page, and tap **Add** on what you want. You can mix and match – for example:

- Cleanup + eyebrow threading + full arms waxing
- [Pedicure](/manicure-pedicure-at-home-indore/) + manicure + head massage
- A gold [facial at home](/facial-cleanup-at-home-indore/) a few days before a function, then [party makeup](/makeup-artist-at-home-indore/) on the day

Your estimated total updates as you add services. You can [see every price before you book](/home-salon-price-list-indore/) – nothing is hidden until the end.

**Tip:** your selection is saved in your browser while you move between pages, so you can add a facial from one page and waxing from another.

## Step 2: Tap Book Now and fill in your details

The booking form asks for:

- **Name** and **10-digit mobile number**
- **Area or locality** in Indore (for example, Vijay Nagar, Scheme No. 78, Rajendra Nagar)
- **Preferred date** – any day up to 90 days ahead
- **Preferred time** – slots every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 8 PM

Check the [localities we cover](/areas-we-serve-indore/). If your area isn't listed, enter it anyway – our team will tell you on WhatsApp whether a beautician is available.

## Step 3: Use Additional Instructions well

This optional box makes a real difference. Mention:

- **Skin or health notes:** sensitive skin, acne, allergies to any product, recent skin treatment
- **Number of people:** if your mother or sister is also booking, list who gets what
- **Occasion and deadline:** "wedding function at 7 PM, need to be ready by 5:30"
- **Preferences:** a particular brow shape or nail colour, or that you'd like your own makeup products used
- **Directions:** landmark, gate number, tower and floor

## Step 4: Send the message on WhatsApp

When you submit the form, WhatsApp opens with a ready message containing your details, services, estimated total, date and time. **Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.**

If WhatsApp doesn't open (some browsers block pop-ups), the page shows a button to open it again.

## Step 5: Wait for confirmation

Your WhatsApp message is a **booking request**, not a confirmed booking. The appointment is confirmed only when the Ready2Glow team replies to confirm your beautician and time. If your chosen slot doesn't work, reply on the same WhatsApp chat to sort out another time.

## How to get ready for your appointment

You don't need much. The beautician brings the products, towels and disposable items. From your side:

| Service | Keep ready |
|---|---|
| Facial or cleanup | A bed or reclining chair, a pillow, access to water |
| Waxing | A bed or a spot where you can lie down; hair about 4–6 mm long |
| Pedicure / manicure | A chair and warm water |
| Hair spa | Access to water to rinse; no need to wash hair beforehand |
| Hair cut | Clean, dry hair is preferred |
| Makeup and saree draping | Your outfit, jewellery and footwear; good light near a mirror |

A few more tips:

- Choose a room with good light and a plug point nearby.
- Don't apply heavy cream or makeup on the areas being treated.
- Keep pets in another room during the appointment.

## How much notice do you need?

- **Weddings and festival dates:** book 3–7 days in advance.
- **Other makeup bookings:** 1–2 days' notice is usually enough.
- **Regular services** like cleanup, waxing, threading or mani-pedi: pick your preferred date and time, and our team will confirm availability on WhatsApp.

## Payment, changes and the final price

Website prices are estimates. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp. Questions about payment or changing your slot? Ask us on WhatsApp.

### Can I book without using the website form?

Yes. You can message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 88203 69048 with the services, your area, date and time. The form simply writes that message for you.

### Is my booking confirmed when I send the WhatsApp message?

No. It's a request. Your appointment is confirmed only after our team replies to confirm the beautician's availability for your slot.

### Can I book services for two or three people in one visit?

Yes. Add the services for each person and mention the number of people in Additional Instructions.

### Is my phone number stored on your website?

No. The details you enter are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. They're shared with us only if you press Send.
