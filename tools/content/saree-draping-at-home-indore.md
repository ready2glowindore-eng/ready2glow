## Draping style

## What to keep ready before your appointment

- Your saree, ironed, with the fall and pico done
- A well-fitted blouse and petticoat (tie the petticoat firmly – it holds the pleats)
- The footwear you'll wear at the function, so the length is set correctly
- A few safety pins, just in case
- A full-length mirror, if you have one

Wear your jewellery and finish your hair before draping where possible, so the pallu isn't disturbed later. Plan a little extra time for heavy silks or a style you haven't worn before.
