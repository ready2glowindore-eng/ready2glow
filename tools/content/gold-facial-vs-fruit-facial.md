**Short answer:** choose a **Fruit Facial** for routine care, and a **Gold Facial** when you want to look your best for an occasion. Neither one is "better" for every woman. It depends on what the facial is for.

## What both facials include

At Ready2Glow, both facials follow the same core steps:

1. Skin check and cleansing
2. Scrub and steam to open pores
3. Massage with facial cream
4. Face pack or mask
5. Toner and moisturiser to finish

The difference is in the products used and the extra time. The Gold Facial takes about 15 minutes longer, with more time for massage and the pack.

## Fruit facial: gentle care for everyday skin

A fruit facial uses fruit-based creams and packs. It's light and refreshing, and suits most skin types. That's why many women choose it as their **monthly facial**.

**Pick a Fruit Facial if:**

- Your skin looks dull or tired and you want a refresh
- You want a facial every month without spending too much
- Your skin is sensitive and you prefer something simple (mention it when you book)

## Gold facial: for occasions

A gold facial uses gold-based creams and packs and feels more indulgent. It's a popular choice before weddings, receptions and festivals.

To be honest about it: the visible glow after any facial comes mostly from **cleansing, exfoliation, massage and hydration**. There isn't strong scientific evidence that gold itself changes your skin. What you're really paying for is a longer, more pampering session at the right time before your event.

**Pick a Gold Facial if:**

- You have a wedding, reception, party or festival coming up
- You want the longer, more relaxing session
- You don't get facials often and want to treat yourself

## Which facial is best for dry skin in winter?

Indore winters are dry, and many women notice tight, flaky skin from November onwards. For **dry skin in winter**, both facials can help, because the massage and moisturising steps add hydration. Some simple guidance:

- **For regular winter care,** a Fruit Facial once a month, plus daily moisturiser, is usually enough.
- **Before a winter wedding or function,** a Gold Facial 2–3 days before gives you the longer, more nourishing session.
- **Tell your beautician your skin is dry** so the scrub can be gentler and more time goes into massage and moisture.
- **Skip the D-Tan pack in winter** unless you've actually been in the sun a lot. It's meant for tan, not dryness.

At home between facials, use a gentle cleanser instead of a harsh face wash, moisturise twice a day, and keep using sunscreen. Winter sun still tans.

## Which facial is best for oily skin, acne or glowing skin?

- **Oily skin:** a [cleanup](/blog/cleanup-vs-facial/) every 3–4 weeks is often enough on its own. Add a Fruit Facial when your skin looks dull.
- **Acne-prone skin:** avoid heavy massage on active pimples. Mention acne when you book, and for persistent acne or pigmentation, see a dermatologist. A salon facial is care, not treatment.
- **For a glow before an event:** a Gold Facial 2–3 days before is the most common choice.

## Timing: when to book your facial

- **Never on the day of an event, and avoid the day before.** Skin can look a little pink after steam and massage. 2–3 days before is ideal.
- **For regular care,** once a month is plenty. See [how often to get a facial](/blog/how-often-facial-waxing-threading/).
- **Getting married?** Fit the Gold Facial into your [pre-wedding beauty timeline](/blog/pre-wedding-beauty-timeline/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Gold facial or fruit facial – which is better?

Neither is better for everyone. A Fruit Facial (₹699, about 60 minutes) suits regular monthly care. A Gold Facial (₹999, about 75 minutes) is the popular choice 2–3 days before weddings, parties and festivals.

### Is fruit facial good for skin?

Yes, for most skin types it's a gentle, refreshing facial. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, mention it when you book so suitable products can be chosen.

### Is gold facial good for skin?

It's a pleasant, nourishing facial and a popular pre-event choice. Most of the glow comes from the cleansing, massage and hydration steps rather than from the gold itself.

### Which facial is best for dry skin in winter?

A Fruit Facial once a month suits regular winter care. Before a function, a Gold Facial 2–3 days before gives a longer, more nourishing session. Tell your beautician your skin is dry.

### Can I get a facial the day before a wedding?

It's better not to. Book it 2–3 days before so any redness settles.
