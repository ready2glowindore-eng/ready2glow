Manicure and pedicure: what's the difference?

The steps are similar. The difference is where they're done.

  • Manicure comes from the Latin words for hand and care. It covers your hands and fingernails.
  • Pedicure comes from the words for foot and care. It covers your feet and toenails, and usually takes longer because feet need more work on hard skin and heels.

Manicure aur pedicure mein kya antar hai? Simple: manicure = haath aur nakhun ki dekhbhal, pedicure = pair aur nakhun ki dekhbhal.

What happens in a manicure

A Ready2Glow manicure at home takes about 40 minutes:

  1. Old polish removed and nails shaped
  2. Hands soaked in warm water
  3. Cuticle care and hand scrub
  4. Relaxing hand massage
  5. Nail polish of your choice

What happens in a pedicure

A pedicure takes about 50 minutes:

  1. Old polish removed and toenails shaped
  2. Feet soaked in warm water
  3. Cuticle care, scrub and callus care for rough heels and hard skin
  4. Relaxing foot massage
  5. Nail polish of your choice

Spa pedicure vs regular pedicure

A spa pedicure includes everything in a regular pedicure, plus a mask and a longer massage. It takes about 60 minutes instead of 50.

Choose a regular Pedicure (₹549) if you want neat, clean feet as part of your monthly routine.

Choose a Spa Pedicure (₹799) if:

  • Your heels are dry or rough, especially in winter
  • Your feet are tired from standing or walking all day
  • You have a wedding or function coming up
  • You simply want the more relaxing version

Benefits of a manicure and pedicure

Being realistic, a manicure and pedicure:

  • Keep nails neat, clean and evenly shaped
  • Soften rough skin on heels and around nails with soaking, scrubbing and callus care
  • Feel relaxing, thanks to the massage
  • Give polished, finished hands and feet for events

They're grooming and self-care, not medical treatment. If you have a nail fungus, an ingrown toenail that's painful, a wound, or diabetes with foot problems, see a doctor before booking a pedicure.

How often should you get them?

  • Manicure: every 2–3 weeks, as polish and cuticles grow out
  • Pedicure: every 3–4 weeks, and a Spa Pedicure in winter if your heels crack easily

Yes, if you can. Manicure plus Pedicure takes about 1.5 hours at home and finishes both hands and feet in one go. The Monthly Pamper package combines Fruit Facial, Full Legs Waxing, Manicure and Pedicure in one visit. See beauty packages.

What you need at home

Just a chair to sit on and access to warm water. The beautician brings the tub, tools, products and towels. Metal tools are sanitised before every appointment, and disposable items such as files are fresh for each customer. More on manicure and pedicure at home in Indore.

Tips:

  • Remove any gel or acrylic nails before the appointment, or mention them when you book.
  • Have your polish colour in mind.
  • Let polish dry fully afterwards. Avoid water for about 30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between manicure and pedicure?

A manicure is for your hands and fingernails. A pedicure is for your feet and toenails and includes callus care for rough heels. A Ready2Glow manicure takes about 40 minutes and a pedicure about 50.

What is a spa pedicure?

A pedicure with a mask and a longer massage for extra relaxation. At Ready2Glow it takes about 60 minutes and costs ₹799.

Is a spa pedicure worth it?

If your heels are dry or rough, your feet are tired, or you have an event coming up, many women find it worth the extra ₹250 over a regular pedicure. For routine care, a regular pedicure is enough.

Can I get a pedicure at home?

Yes. You just need a chair and warm water. The beautician brings the tub, tools, products and towels.

How long does a manicure and pedicure take together?

About 1.5 hours: around 40 minutes for the manicure and 50 for the pedicure.
