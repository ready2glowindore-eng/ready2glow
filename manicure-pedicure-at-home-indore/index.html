  • Moisturise hands and feet daily.
  • Wear gloves while washing dishes to protect your nails.

Manicure & Pedicure at Home – FAQs

What do I need to arrange for a pedicure at home?

A chair to sit on and access to warm water. Our beautician brings the tub, tools, products and towels.

Are the tools sanitised?

Yes. Metal tools are sanitised before every appointment and disposable items such as files are fresh for each customer.

How long does a manicure and pedicure take?

A manicure takes about 40 minutes and a pedicure about 50 minutes. A spa pedicure takes around an hour.
