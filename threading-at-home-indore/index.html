How It Works at Home

  1. Discuss the brow shape you want
  2. Skin cleaned and lightly powdered
  3. Hair removed with fresh cotton thread
  4. Soothing toner or aloe gel applied

Aftercare Tips

  • Avoid makeup on the threaded area for a few hours.
  • Skip facials, scrubs and direct sun for 24 hours.
  • Apply aloe vera gel if you notice slight redness.

Threading at Home – FAQs

How often should I get my eyebrows threaded?

Every 2–3 weeks keeps your brows in shape, depending on how fast your hair grows.

Can I book threading together with other services?

Yes. Threading is a popular add-on with a cleanup, facial or waxing in the same home appointment.

Is a new thread used for every customer?

Yes, a fresh cotton thread is used for every customer.
