Home Salon & Beauty Parlour Services in Indore

Ready2Glow is a home beauty service in Indore that brings the parlour to your doorstep. Instead of travelling across the city and waiting at a salon, you choose your services online, pick a time that suits you, and a trained beautician arrives at your home with a complete, sanitised kit.

We offer facial & cleanup, waxing, threading, manicure & pedicure, hair spa & hair care, makeup artist – everything you would expect from a good beauty parlour, delivered at home. Whether you need a quick eyebrow threading, a monthly waxing and pedicure routine, a glowing facial before a function, or party makeup and saree draping for a wedding, you can book it all in one appointment.

Booking is simple: add the services you want, fill in your name, area and preferred slot, and WhatsApp opens with your booking request ready to send. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a home beauty service with Ready2Glow?

Add the services you want, tap Book Now, fill in your name, phone, area, preferred date and time, and WhatsApp opens with your complete booking request. Just press Send and our team will confirm availability.

Which areas of Indore do you cover?

We provide home beauty services across Indore, including Vijay Nagar, Palasia, Bhawarkuan, Nipania, Rau, Annapurna, Sudama Nagar, Rajendra Nagar and nearby localities.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the WhatsApp message?

Your WhatsApp message is a booking request. Your appointment is confirmed only after the Ready2Glow team replies to confirm the beautician's availability for your chosen slot.

Are the products hygienic and safe?

Yes. Our beauticians carry sanitised tools and use single-use items such as wax strips, gloves and disposable sheets for every appointment.

Can I book multiple services in one appointment?

Yes. Select as many services or packages as you like and they will all be included in a single WhatsApp booking request with the estimated total.
