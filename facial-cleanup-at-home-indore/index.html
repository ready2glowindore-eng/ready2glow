Aftercare Tips

  • Avoid direct sun and heavy makeup for 24 hours.
  • Don't use scrubs or face wash with strong actives for 2 days.
  • Drink plenty of water and keep your skin moisturised.
  • Book your facial 2–3 days before an event, not on the same day.

Facial & Cleanup at Home – FAQs

How often should I get a facial or cleanup?

A cleanup every 3–4 weeks is ideal for most people. A facial such as fruit or gold facial can be done once a month or before special occasions.

Is a home facial suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Tell us about sensitivity, allergies or acne in the booking form's additional instructions and our beautician will choose suitable products and skip harsh steps.

What do I need to arrange at home?

Just a bed or reclining chair, a pillow and access to water. Our beautician brings the products, towels and disposable items.

How long does a facial at home take?

A cleanup takes about 45 minutes and a facial between 60 and 75 minutes, depending on the service you choose.
